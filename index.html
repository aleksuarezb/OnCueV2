Help & User Guide

  1. Pick your role. MD can add songs, build setlists, and push a song to everyone. Viewer follows along automatically once something's been pushed.
  2. Add a song (MD only): Library → "+ New Song". Enter a title, then add one or more chart versions — type the chart exactly as it should look on stage. A version named "Numbers" is shown by default.
  3. Build a setlist (MD only): Setlists → "+ New Setlist" → add songs from the library, reorder with the arrows.
  4. Browsing is private. Swiping or tapping through songs — in a setlist or from the Library — only moves your own screen. Nothing is sent to anyone else until you explicitly push.
  5. Push to Everyone (MD only): the button on the chart screen sends exactly the song you're looking at to every Viewer who's following, asking you to confirm first. Tap it again ("Stop Live") to stop. Turn the confirmation off in Settings (⚙) if you'd rather it push instantly.
  6. Current Key (in setlist mode): the badge beside the song title is the key the whole band is playing this song in, regardless of anyone's capo. MD can tap it to change it; Viewers see it read-only. Every device sees this, independent of what's pushed live. It's separate from...
  7. Chart Key (top-right dropdown): pick a different chart version for yourself — e.g. a capo chart — without changing what anyone else sees. Your choice carries over automatically to any later song that has a matching version.
  8. Navigate: swipe left/right on the chart, or use the arrows on the right edge — first through a long song's own pages, then on to the next setlist song.
  9. Force a column break: if a verse gets split awkwardly across a column, put a line containing just --- right before it in the chart text — it forces a break there and never shows on stage.
  10. Chart modes: tap the ☀︎ icon on the chart screen to switch between dark and light display.
  11. Voice search: tap 🎤 (Library or chart screen) and say a song title or a line of its lyrics — it opens that song for you. Just for you; it doesn't push to anyone.
  12. Switch identity anytime by tapping the OnCue logo in the header.
